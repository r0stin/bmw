How the numbers are built

Value

  • Value = today's value × age factor × kilometre factor. The kilometre factor comes from a fit across seven 2016 228i xDrive coupes listed in Canada in September 2026, from 32,265 km ($23,995) to 215,612 km ($9,995). Price falls about 0.47% per 1,000 km, and the fit puts a 56,000 km car near $22,000 at a dealer. Your $18,500 is about 84% of that, in line with a private sale.
  • The 5% per year for age alone comes from Kelley Blue Book's 32% three-year drop for this model, minus the part explained by typical US kilometres.

Fuel

  • NRCan rated the 2016 228i xDrive at 10.4 L/100 km city and 6.8 highway; the EPA's tougher cycle gives 10.2 and 6.9. The model uses real-world 11.2 city and 7.3 highway, adds 4% for Ontario winters, then applies your driving style.
  • Premium at $2.139/L is the GTA posted price on Sept 28, 2026, after a 7-cent drop. Prices are elevated compared with earlier in 2026.

Maintenance and repairs

  • Costs are Ontario independent-shop prices in CAD including 13% HST, built from US parts and labour quotes converted at 1.41 and cross-checked against Canadian listings. Examples: N20 timing chain service runs US$1,890–2,890 at a specialist; a BMW OEM water pump is about US$450 before labour.
  • Each repair's chance rises along a curve of kilometres plus a per-year aging allowance for rubber and plastic parts. Each is counted once during your plan. Small unlisted fixes (sensors, bulbs, a window regulator) add about $320 a year, rising 7% a year and with distance.
  • RepairPal puts the 228i xDrive's average upkeep at US$920 a year across all ages; CarEdge puts the 2 Series at about US$13,780 over its first 10 years with a 42% chance of a major repair. Years 11 onward cost more, which is where your car is now.

What it leaves out

  • Collisions, deductibles, parking, washes and financing. Ontario no longer charges licence plate sticker fees. Selling costs (safety certificate, used vehicle package) are under $200.
  • Car values and repair odds are estimates, not quotes. Treat the heatmap as a range of about ±10%.
